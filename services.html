Water-based & vegan

01 // APPAREL

Direct-to-Garment (DTG)

Perfect for complex designs with many colors. The ink is sprayed directly onto the garment and soaks into the fibers, creating a remarkably soft feel and excellent durability.

  • No color limits or minimum orders
  • Highly detailed, photorealistic prints
  • Best for T-shirts, Hoodies, and Sweatshirts
02 // ALL-OVER

Sublimation Printing

Using extreme heat, the ink turns into a gas that permanently bonds with the fabric or coating. The result is a vibrant, indestructible print that will never crack, peel, or fade.

  • True edge-to-edge printing capabilities
  • Vibrant, punchy colors that pop
  • Best for Mugs, Phone Cases, and Activewear
Heat Pressed

Never fades or peels

Premium Threads

High tensile strength

03 // TEXTURED

Premium Embroidery

Give your brand a premium, textured look. Our state-of-the-art embroidery machines use highly durable threads to stitch your design directly into the fabric.

  • Professional, high-end 3D look
  • Extreme durability that outlasts the garment
  • Best for Hats, Polos, Jackets, and Bags
